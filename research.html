Cyclic peptides that block fertilization

KumarLab · PI: Dr. Vivek Kumar · 2023–present

Question
Can a cyclic peptide be designed to interrupt sperm–egg recognition selectively enough to work as a non-hormonal male contraceptive?
Approach
Machine-learning-guided design of cyclic peptides against sperm-specific protein targets, followed by docking and refinement.
Where it stands
Manuscript and patent application both in preparation. Presented at the AAP/ASCI/APSA Joint Meeting and the National Contraception Meeting; took first place at the NJIT Honors Interdisciplinary Research Forum.
